## Camp Bow Wow — `Camp_Bow_Wow_Takeoff_EXAMPLE.pdf` (rendered: `images/CampBowWow_A2.0.png`)

Dog daycare/boarding tenant improvement (JcM Design Group). This is the **house style** for a
marked-up-plan takeoff:

- The real Finish Floor Plan (A2.0) is color-flooded **by finish system**, each keyed to the finish
  legend, with the **SF total per system boxed on the plan**:
  - **Red = F6 "Permatek Tempset" epoxy floor finish = 5,615 SF** — the resinous-flooring scope
    (Groom 111, Prep 112, Enrichment Yards 113–116, Kennel Yards 117–120, Corridor 121).
  - Green = F1 porcelain tile = 120 SF · Blue = F5 vinyl plank = 890 SF (other trades).
- Scope conflicts are flagged directly on the sheet ("Flooring callout conflicts at Kennel Yard
  117 & 119 — pricing reflects F6").

For our trade this is an **epoxy/resinous** job (kennel flooring: seamless, sanitary, slip- and
chemical-resistant, integral cove) — the epoxy counterpart to the polished-concrete work in
`knowledge-base/03-epoxy-resinous-systems.md`. If it becomes a live bid, note the likely
integral-cove-vs-rubber-base conflict (finish schedule shows rope rubber base B2; kennels normally
want integral epoxy cove) and confirm floor drains/slopes — both are RFIs.
