## 2. POLISHED CONCRETE system (Sheet A9.0 Concrete Specs §3.07 — Euclid Chemical)

Applied by an **Euclid-trained applicator** (GC must source from Euclid's approved list — Phil
Brandt, Euclid Chemical 877-438-3826). Same applicator does joint fill + densifier + polish.

**7-step grind/polish process:**
1. Grind — combo **60 + 100 grit** resin-bond diamonds (not pads), each pass 50% overlap
2. Densify — **Euco Diamond Hard** liquid densifier/sealer @ **225 SF/gal**
3. Polish — combo **100 + 200 grit** resin-bond diamonds, head pressure ≥150 PSI
4. Polish — **400 grit** resin-bond diamonds
5. Densify — **Euco Diamond Hard** @ **700 SF/gal** (2nd application)
6. Burnish — **800 grit** diamond-impregnated pads
7. Burnish — **1500 grit** diamond-impregnated pads

- **Gloss result:** SOGV (overall) **≥35**, SMGV (minimum) **≥30**, per **Horiba IG-320**; 4 readings
  at 90° per location; "mid-level gloss." Report to GC within 24 hr.
- **Color:** natural gray (no dye).
- **Joint/crack fill:** semi-rigid polyurea **"QwikJoint UVR"**, full width & depth, ≥1" deep, no
  backer rod. **Included up to 1,150 LF** (finish-plan note). Control joints ≤13' o.c. (4 equal
  spaces typ.) → actual LF may exceed 1,150; overage is a unit-price add. Applicator to include a
  per-LF cost to make one return trip if sidewall separation occurs (spec).
- **Slab:** interior SOG **4,000 psi**, cured with **Kurez DR VOx / DR 100** dissipating cure (must
  be removed with **Euco Clean & Strip** before polishing). Trowel finish, FF/FL tolerances by GC.
- **Sequence gate:** joint fill / densifier / polish may not start until building is fully enclosed.

## 4. EPOXY RESINOUS system (Sheet A9.0 — Increte flake, by Euclid Chemical)

**Locations:** Pet Wash (138 SF) + Men's & Women's restrooms (~150 SF est.). Decorative flake
(broadcast-to-refusal) system with integral cove.

- **Surface prep:** mechanically abrade to **CSP 2-3** (ICRI 310.2); check pH per ASTM D4262
- a. **Initial coat:** "Increte High Performance Epoxy" **GRAY**, 1-gal kit @ **120 SF/gal** (notched squeegee)
- b. **Broadcast:** "Increte Granite Coat Chips" (**Mica**) to refusal, then vacuum
- c. **Grout coat:** "Increte High Performance Epoxy" **CLEAR**, 1-gal kit @ **120 SF/gal**
- d. **Wear coat:** "Increte Polyseal Polyaspartic" **CLEAR**, 2-gal kit @ **120 SF/gal**
- **4" integral epoxy cove base** at FRP/cement-board wall transition (epoxy + trowelable aggregate,
  chips broadcast, then grout + wear coat) — seamless, at perimeter of each epoxy room.

## 6. Risk / coordination

- **Applicator qualification:** must be on Euclid's approved-applicator list — bid gate.
- **Vinyl-plank "expanded clothing area"** overlays Retail Sales; extent set by final fixture plan
  (8 wks before fixtures). Polished SF may **deduct** by that area → RFI/alternate.
- **Joint fill overage** beyond 1,150 LF → unit price ($/LF).
- **Slab, cure method, FF/FL flatness** by GC/concrete sub — polish quality depends on it; confirm
  dissipating cure was used (non-dissipating cure = extra removal cost).
- **Moisture:** new penetrating-densifier polish is not moisture-sensitive like a coating; epoxy
  rooms need pH check (ASTM D4262) and CSP 2-3 prep.
- Restroom SF (~150) is estimated — confirm from enlarged plans (A7.x). Pet Wash is a wet area
  (FRP walls, floor drain, aluminum mesh) — coordinate drain/slope.
- Vinyl plank (offices + expanded clothing) = other trade; excluded.
