# Scope Letter — Tractor Supply Co. Chino Valley, AZ

**Project:** TSC Q4-2023 Prototype, 760 N. AZ-89, Chino Valley, AZ
**Trades:** Polished concrete + epoxy resinous flooring (Sheet A9.0 Concrete Specs; Finish Schedule A8.0)
**Bid date:** [TBD]
**Documents:** Architectural Plan Set dated 2026-06-26 (A2.0 Code Analysis, A2.1 Floor Plan, A8.0
Finish Schedule, A9.0 Concrete Specs); Prototype set; SOV. This proposal is based on these documents.

## Inclusions
1. **Polished concrete** — Retail Sales + Vestibule, **≈15,885 SF** — Euclid 7-step ground/polish
   process (60/100 grind → densify → 100/200 → 400 → densify → 800 burnish → 1500 burnish) to
   **SOGV ≥35 / SMGV ≥30** (Horiba IG-320), natural gray.
2. Two applications of **Euco Diamond Hard** densifier/sealer.
3. **Semi-rigid polyurea joint/crack fill (QwikJoint UVR)** — **up to 1,150 LF** included.
4. Removal of dissipating cure (Euco Clean & Strip) prior to polishing.
5. Edge/hand grinding at walls and columns (≈700 LF).
6. Test polish/mock-up; gloss testing and reporting (Horiba IG-320).
7. **Epoxy resinous flooring** — Pet Wash + restrooms, **≈290 SF** — Increte flake system (gray
   base + Granite Coat chips + clear grout + Polyseal polyaspartic wear coat) over CSP 2-3 prep.
8. **4" integral epoxy cove base** (≈110 LF) at epoxy rooms.
9. Finished-floor protection; dust control/HEPA; slurry disposal.
10. One mobilization; pre-installation conference attendance; manufacturer rep present for 1st
    densifier application.

## Alternate / related (price on request)
- **Ground-&-sealed concrete** — Stockroom + Feed Storage, **5,053 SF** (1-cut grind + Euco Diamond
  Hard). Same applicator; separate line.

## Exclusions
1. Concrete slab, placement, cure, and **FF/FL flatness tolerances** (by GC / concrete sub).
2. Concrete replacement, trench pour-backs, patching of holes ≥3" (GC per finish note 9).
3. **Vinyl plank** flooring (offices + "expanded clothing area") and all other floor finishes — other trades.
4. Floor drains, slope-to-drain, plumbing (Pet Wash) — by others.
5. FRP wall panels / cement board substrate for epoxy cove — by others (we cove to it).
6. Moisture mitigation (not required for penetrating-densifier polish; epoxy pH check included).
7. Temporary heat/power/water/enclosure; final cleaning beyond our work.

2. **Polished SF may deduct** the vinyl-plank "expanded clothing area" once the final fixture plan is
   issued (keynote 33). Deduct at **$__/SF**.
3. **Joint fill beyond 1,150 LF: ADD $__/LF.** Return trip to refill separated joints: **$__/LF**.
4. Price assumes a **dissipating cure** (Kurez DR VOx/DR100) was used; a non-dissipating cure or

7. Quantities are approximate from schematic areas; contract on field-verified measure.
