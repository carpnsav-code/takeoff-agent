## 5. Existing-slab risk items (drive contingency & unit prices)

1. **Carpet + adhesive removal.** Carpet demo is listed under "Demolition" (likely GC/others), but
   **residual adhesive/mastic removal and grinding the slab clean enough to polish is the risk.**
   Old adhesive can require aggressive metal-bond cutting and can telegraph/stain. Qualify: carpet
   demo by others; adhesive removal by us as prep (unit price) or excluded.
2. **Unknown slab condition** under 20+ yr carpet — cracks, patches, gouges, control-joint spalls,
   past repairs, possible prior coatings. Spec provides overlay/topping and patch options precisely
   because condition is unknown. Carry patch allowance + **exclude overlay topping (unit price
   add)**.
3. **Moisture.** Existing slab-on-grade, unknown vapor retarder. RH/MVER/pH testing required;
   exceedance forces remediation for warranty. **Mitigation excluded from base — unit price add.**
4. **Aggregate exposure undefined** (mock-up driven). Deeper exposure = more metal-bond passes =
   more cost. Base bid assumes light/cream ("salt & pepper") exposure consistent with Level 3 / DOI
   50–65 / no dye; deeper exposure via mock-up is an add. **RFI.**
5. **Flatness telegraphing** — Level 3 gloss on a 20-yr slab will show waviness; not our defect but
   qualify appearance.
