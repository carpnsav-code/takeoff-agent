# Scope Letter — Nordstrom Rack #770 "Last Chance" — Polished Concrete

**Project:** Nordstrom Rack #770, 1919 East Camelback Road, Phoenix, AZ 85016
**Trade:** Polished Concrete Finishing (CSI 033536) and related slab items (030133)
**Bid date:** [TBD]
**Documents:** Drawing SD02 Schematic Design Plan (rev 05/28/26); ER-1 First Floor Plan (existing,
for reference); SOW R1 dated 06/23/26 (Specs 030133 & 033536, V3.1 04/14/2026). **This proposal is
based on these documents only.** SD02 is schematic; quantities are approximate pending a
dimensioned/CAD plan.

## Inclusions
1. Grind and polish existing concrete sales floor to **Level 3 finish (800-grit), DOI 50–65 per
   ASTM D5767, specular gloss ≥35 per ASTM D523, natural gray (no dye)** — approx. **18,000 SF**
   (estimated; see qualification 1).
2. Full diamond grind/hone/polish sequence per 033536 (metal 40/80/150, hybrid, resin
   100/200/400/800), including edge/hand grinding flush to walls and columns.
3. Low-VOC silicate hardener/densifier applied to rejection.
4. Topical, non-film-forming finishing sealer.
5. Semi-rigid polyurea (Shore A75) fill of existing control/construction joints, colored to match
   gray concrete, completed ≥72 hours before grinding.
6. Active-crack fill allowance: **300 LF** (semi-rigid filler).
7. Surface imperfection patch/repair allowance: **500 SF** (tinted Rapid Set Cement All).
8. One 10×10 ft mock-up with saw-cut joint and joint filler.
9. Four (4) brass department markers plus one marker mock-up.
10. Substrate acceptance testing: ASTM F2170 (RH), F1869 (MVER), F710 (pH), with notification of
    exceedances per spec.
11. Field coefficient-of-friction test by qualified walkway auditor (allowance).
12. Dust control/containment with HEPA vacuum and pre-separator; slurry/dust disposal per AHJ.
13. Finished-floor protection with kraft curing paper.
14. Submittals, closeout, maintenance instruction, manufacturer warranty, and 5-gal extra-stock
    cleaning agent.
15. Two (2) mobilizations assumed (early/BOH area, then main sales floor).

## Exclusions
1. **Carpet removal / demolition** of existing floor finishes (by others). We include residual
   adhesive removal only as noted — see qualification 3.
2. **Moisture mitigation / vapor barrier** if testing exceeds spec limits (RH >75%, MVER >8 lb, pH
   out of 8–10) — see unit price.
3. **Polishing overlay / self-leveling topping** if the existing slab is unsuitable or requires
   leveling — see unit price.
4. **Asbestos or hazardous-material** testing, abatement, or disposal (e.g., asbestos-containing
   carpet mastic).
5. Structural concrete, new slab placement, trenches/block-outs/infills, vapor retarder under new
   slab, saw-cutting new joints, and slope correction (Section 030133 / concrete or GC scope,
   except polish-related patching).
6. Polishing of BOH office / non-sales areas (pending RFI-02).
7. Repair/leveling of slab beyond the stated allowances (crack fill 300 LF, patch 500 SF).
8. Protection of our finished work by other trades; final cleaning beyond our own work.
9. Temporary power (480V/3-phase), water, lighting, heat, barricades, dumpsters, and after-hours
   mall access fees unless listed.
10. Premium/off-hours labor unless required and identified by RFI response.
11. Bonds and permits (or: bond included at __%).
