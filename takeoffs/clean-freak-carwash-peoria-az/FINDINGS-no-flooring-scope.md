# Findings — Clean Freak Car Wash (Circle K), Peoria AZ — NO polish/epoxy scope in this set

**Project:** Circle K "Clean Freak" car wash + c-store — raze & rebuild (GC bid)
**Location:** El Mirage Rd & Vistancia Blvd, Peoria, AZ
**Owner:** Circle K Stores Inc. · **Civil/AE:** HFA
**Bids due:** July 24, 2026, 3:00 pm · **RFIs due:** July 10, 2026
**Reviewed:** 2026-07-10

## Verdict
**No polished-concrete or epoxy/resinous flooring scope is evident in the documents provided, and
the architectural finish package is not in the set.** No takeoff was produced. Two reasons:

1. **Our trade isn't in the finish scope shown.** The **Responsibility Matrix** lists the interior
   floors as **"Sales Floor Tile"** and **"Restroom Floor Tile"** — i.e., **tile**, not polished
   concrete or epoxy. A keyword sweep of all 10 PDFs found **zero** mentions of polish, epoxy,
   resinous, sealed concrete, densifier, floor coating, or a finish schedule.
2. **The architectural Bid Set is missing from the upload.** Addendum 1 (RFP) says the link
   includes a "Bid Set," but what was provided is the **civil / site subset** only (see below). No
   A-series floor plan or finish schedule to take off from even if there were flooring scope.

The geotech "tunnel" references are the **car-wash tunnel structure/soils**, not a floor finish.

## What we'd need to confirm/produce a flooring takeoff
- The **architectural Bid Set**: floor plan + **finish schedule / finish legend**, and any car-wash
  **equipment/mechanical room** or **tunnel** floor-finish spec.
- If Circle K's prototype puts an **epoxy/urethane coating** in the car-wash equipment room or a
  **sealed/polished** floor in the c-store back-of-house, that would be our scope — but it is **not
  documented in these files**. On the current documents, the finished floors are tile (other trade).

## Recommended next step
Ask Circle K (or pull from the Blue Book link) the **architectural drawings + finish schedule**. If
those confirm tile-only floors, this ITB has **no work for our trade** and should be marked
*no-bid* for polished concrete/epoxy.
