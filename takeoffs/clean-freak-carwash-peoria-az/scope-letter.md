**Bid due:** July 24, 2026 · **RFIs due:** July 10, 2026
**Documents:** Sheet A120 Floor Finish Plan & Finish Legend (REV 1, 3/26/2026); Addendum 1 (RFP),

pricing per RFP (no lump sums).

## Inclusions
1. **EPXY-2 epoxy flooring — Dur-A-Flex "Dur-A-Quartz One," gray** (broadcast quartz epoxy system:
   primer + quartz broadcast + grout + seal/topcoat) in **Office 100, Unisex Restroom 103, Break
   Room 104, Storage 105** — **485 SF** (measured from A100).
2. Surface preparation: mechanical profile to the Dur-A-Flex-required CSP (2–3) + cleaning + pH/
   moisture check.
3. Terminations at doorways/transitions to adjacent broom-finish concrete (≈40 LF).
4. Mock-up/sample if required; one mobilization; dust control and finished-floor protection.

## Exclusions
1. **CS-1 exposed concrete (grey broom finish)** — Mechanical 107, Electrical 108, **Wash Tunnel
   109**, Fire Riser 110 — by concrete sub, **not our trade**.
2. **Wall base** — rubber base (RBR-1) and tile base (MT-1) — by resilient/tile trade (see RFI-03).
3. Slab, cure, moisture mitigation (unless required by test — unit price), floor drains/trench
   drain, slope-to-drain, CMU sealing, Nuform wall panels — by others.
4. Tile (T-1), wall finishes/paint, and all non-epoxy finishes — other trades.
5. Fuel system, sitework, dry utilities — not our scope.

5. Work starts after building is enclosed and slab is ready; coordinate with GC schedule.
