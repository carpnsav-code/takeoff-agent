**Finish legend:**
- **EPXY-2 = "Dur-A-Quartz One" epoxy flooring by Dur-A-Flex, color GRAY (ref. structural).**
  Dur-A-Flex Dur-A-Quartz is a **broadcast quartz epoxy system** (primer + quartz broadcast body +

- **CS-1 = exposed concrete, grey with broom finish** (no coating/seal/polish) — concrete sub's
  finish, **not our trade**. (Note: the wash tunnel floor is bare broom-finish CS-1, sloped to
  trench drain — not sealed/coated in this set.)
