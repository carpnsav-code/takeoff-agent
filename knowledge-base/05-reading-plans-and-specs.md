# 05 — Reading Plans & Specs: Where Our Scope Hides

Bid sets bury flooring scope in predictable places. Work through this map for every upload.

## Spec book (project manual)

| Section | Why we read it |
|---|---|
| **03 35 43** Polished Concrete Finishing | Our polish scope: class, gloss, densifier, mock-up, joint fill, protection |
| **09 67 23** Resinous Flooring (.13/.15/.16, VA/UFGS variants) | Our epoxy scope: system build, mils, prep, moisture limits, warranty |
| 03 30 00 Cast-in-Place Concrete | Mix, cure method, flatness FF/FL, finish — all affect polish; cure time affects epoxy schedule |
| 03 01 30 / 03 01 00 Concrete Repair/Maintenance | Repair scope that may be assigned to us |
| 07 92 00 Joint Sealants | Who fills/seals floor joints |
| 09 61 36 / 09 62 00 | Static-resistive flooring, specialty flooring that overlaps |
| 09 05 61.13 Moisture Vapor Emission Control | Standalone mitigation section — check who owns it |
| 02 41 19 / 02 82 00 Demo & Abatement | Existing flooring removal; asbestos mastic = never ours |
| **Division 01** | Mock-ups, phasing, work hours, temporary facilities, LEED submittals, allowances, unit prices requested on bid form |
| 00 series (Instructions to Bidders, Bid Form) | Alternates, unit prices, bid breakdowns we must fill in |

**In each of our sections, extract:** system/manufacturer basis of design + approved equals;
performance standards cited (ASTM/ANSI/CPC); thickness/class/level; colors and patterns; prep and
CSP required; moisture limits and mitigation language; mock-up size/count; testing and QC duties;
warranty term and form; applicator qualifications (years, manufacturer certification, project list —
a bid-blocker if we can't meet it).

## Drawings

| Sheet type | What to pull |
|---|---|
| **A-series finish plans + finish schedule/legend** | The core SF takeoff: which rooms get which finish code; legend decodes codes (e.g., PC-1, RF-2, SC-1) |
| Floor plans (dimensioned) | Measure areas; verify scale against a dimension string before measuring anything |
| Reflected details / interior elevations | **Cove base heights**, wall transitions, wainscot coatings |
| Enlarged plans (kitchens, restrooms, labs) | Drains (EA), slopes, equipment layout, curbs, pits |
| Plumbing plans | Floor drains, trench drains, cleanouts, slab penetrations (count EA/LF) |
| Structural (S-series) | Slab thickness, joints layout (LF of sawcuts!), mix references, slab-on-grade vs elevated, vapor retarder presence |
| Civil/site | Exterior slabs (usually excluded — but confirm) |
| Demo plans | Existing finishes to remove, extent lines |
| Equipment plans (kitchens/industrial) | What sits on the floor; coating under equipment or not; equipment by others timing |
| Phasing/logistics plans | Mobilization count, access, protection |

## Reconciliation checks (each one is an RFI when it fails)

1. Finish schedule room list vs. finish plan hatching — same rooms, same codes?
2. Finish codes on plans vs. systems defined in specs — every code has a spec, every spec'd system
   appears on a plan?
3. "Sealed concrete" or "exposed concrete" on schedule while 03 35 43 exists in the book?
4. Joint fill in 03 35 43 *and* 07 92 00 — double-assigned or unassigned?
5. Room areas in schedule (if listed) vs. measured SF — >5% off, flag it.
6. Addenda incorporated? Check every addendum for spec section reissues and finish plan revisions.
7. Cove base shown in details but absent from finish schedule (or vice versa).
8. Slope-to-drain shown but SL system specified (incompatible — RFI).

## Standard RFI triggers for these trades

- No exposure class or gloss level given for polish (only descriptive words)
- No system thickness or layer build given for resinous ("epoxy paint" ≠ a spec)
- No moisture limit stated, or limit stated but no mitigation assignment
- New slab pour dates vs. required cure time vs. our scheduled start
- Applicator qualification we can't meet as written
- Finish plan/schedule conflicts from the reconciliation list above
- Existing conditions unknowable from documents (coatings, contamination) — request site walk
