# 04 — Surface Prep & Moisture (the make-or-break line items)

Prep and moisture are where flooring bids are won honestly or lost catastrophically. Both trades
share this scope; it is always taken off explicitly, never bundled.

## 1. Concrete Surface Profile (ICRI 310.2R, CSP 1–10)

The spec (or the manufacturer's TDS) will require a profile. Method follows profile:

| CSP | Texture | Typical method | Typical use |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Near-smooth | Diamond grind (fine), acid etch (rare/avoid) | Thin-film coatings, polishing start |
| 3–5 | Light–medium texture | **Shot blast**, medium grind, scarify (5) | SL epoxy, broadcast systems, most 09 67 23 work |
| 6–9 | Heavy texture | Scarification, rotomill | 1/4"+ mortars, urethane cement (5–7 common) |
| 10 | Very rough | Milling/hydrodemolition | Thick toppings, repair |

**Takeoff lines for prep:**
- SF of shot blast / grind / scarify by area (method per required CSP)
- SF of **existing coating, mastic, or curing compound removal** (separate from profiling — removal
  first, then profile)
- LF of hand-grinding at edges, corners, under fixed equipment where machines can't reach
- Spot repairs: allowance for bug holes, spalls, rain-damage — SF or unit price
- **Grease/oil-saturated slabs** (kitchens, industrial): degrease + test + possible deep
  contamination — carry qualification or unit price
- Dust control/vacuums, containment in occupied space

## 2. Moisture testing (ASTM F2170 / F1869)

Excess slab moisture is the #1 cause of resinous flooring failure. The spec will set limits; the
takeoff must record them and price the response.

| Test | Measures | Common spec limit for epoxy |
|---|---|---|
| **ASTM F2170** (in-situ RH probes) | Internal relative humidity at 40% slab depth | 75–80% RH (some systems tolerate 85–90+) |
| **ASTM F1869** (calcium chloride) | Moisture vapor emission rate at surface | 3 lbs / 1000 SF / 24 hr |
| Plastic sheet test (ASTM D4263) | Qualitative screen only | pass/fail indicator |

- F2170 probe count: **3 for the first 1,000 SF + 1 per additional 1,000 SF** → count EA on the takeoff
- Building must be at service temperature/humidity ≥ 48 hrs before testing (schedule risk — flag if
  the schedule shows coating before the building is conditioned)
- Who pays for testing? Spec may assign it to owner's testing agency or to us. State it.

## 3. Moisture mitigation (the contingency that must be priced)

If readings exceed limits, options in ascending cost:

1. Moisture-tolerant primer upgrade (~+$0.50–1.00/SF class of delta)
2. **Epoxy moisture vapor barrier (MVB)** per ASTM F3010 — 100%-solids two-coat or single-coat
   16–20 mil system (~+$2–4/SF class of delta)
3. Wait for slab to dry (schedule cost, rarely available)

**Takeoff treatment — always one of these three, in writing:**
- **Included:** SF of MVB carried in base bid (spec mandates it regardless of readings)
- **Excluded:** "Moisture mitigation excluded; unit price $__/SF if required by test results"
- **Allowance/alternate:** carried as a stated add

New slabs on grade with no vapor retarder, or old slabs with no history, are high-risk — note it.

## 4. Slab repair scope shared by both trades

- Crack routing/filling: LF (existing slabs — walk count or allowance)
- Joint refill (deteriorated fillers in existing warehouses): LF
- Spall/partial-depth repair: SF or EA allowance with unit price
- Divot/bolt-hole patching after equipment demo: EA allowance
- Self-leveling underlayment for out-of-tolerance slabs (only if a flooring flatness spec applies)

## 5. Protection & environmental line items

- Temporary protection of finished floors (Masonite/Ram Board): SF + labor to place/remove
- Protection of adjacent finishes during blasting/grinding (masking, poly): LS or SF
- Climate: most resins need 50°F+ slab and falling-temp dew point margin ≥ 5°F — winter work may
  need temporary heat (whose cost?)
- Power: polishing/grinding equipment needs 480V 3-phase; if no site power, generator rental line
- Water source and slurry disposal for wet polishing (environmental disposal rules — LS)
